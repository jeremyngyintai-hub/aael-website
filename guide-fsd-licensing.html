消防處：牌照處所的消防安全要求與FSI證明書Fire Services Department: Licensing Premises and the FSI Certificate

躍昇建築事務顧問有限公司　·　更新於 2026年7月Ascend Architecture & Engineering Ltd　·　Updated July 2026

食肆、賓館、學校、娛樂場所——幾乎每一類牌照申請，最終都要過消防處呢一關。理解這個制度的運作方式，比死記個別要求更重要，因為他決定了你申請牌照的時間表。Restaurants, guesthouses, schools, entertainment premises — almost every licence application ultimately has to clear the Fire Services Department. Understanding how the system works matters more than memorising individual requirements, because it decides your licensing timeline.

消防處在牌照制度入面的角色

消防處本身不是大部分處所的發牌機關，但幾乎所有需要牌照的處所（食肆、賓館、學校、日間幼兒中心、娛樂場所、按摩院等），發牌機關在批出牌照前都必須先取得消防處的「不反對」意見或者證明書，確認處所的消防安全裝置及走火通道符合要求。

換言之，消防處是整個牌照流程入面的把關者，不是最終發牌人。這個分別好重要——業主或者租客申請牌照被拖慢，好多時原因不在申請表格本身，而是處所的消防安全狀況未達標。

兩份關鍵文件

消防裝置及設備證明書（FS 251）

新建、改動或者加建的消防裝置（例如火警鐘、灑水系統、消防喉轆），完工後須由註冊消防裝置承辦商（FSIC）檢查，確認符合標準後簽發證明書提交消防處存檔。呢份文件是申請牌照嗰陣消防處審視的其中一項核心依據。

消防安全要求（因應處所類別而異）

消防處按處所類別（食肆、賓館、學校等）分別訂明相應的消防安全要求，涵蓋走火通道闊度及數目、防火門、緊急照明、消防裝置類別等。這些要求因處所用途、面積、樓層位置而有分別，並非一套通用標準。

註冊消防裝置承辦商（FSIC）

消防裝置的安裝及保養，法例規定必須由消防處註冊的承辦商進行（分為 Class 1、2、3 等級別，視乎裝置類型）。業主委聘裝修或者消防工程時，應該確認承辦商在消防處的有效註冊名冊之內——這個可以在消防處網站查證。

對牌照申請的實務影響

牌照申請被拖慢，常見原因包括：

- 處所的走火通道被間隔或雜物阻塞，未達消防安全要求 - 消防裝置由未註冊的承辦商安裝，FS 251 無法有效簽發 - 處所用途同原有設計不一致（例如工廈單位改作食肆），消防安全要求隨用途改變而提高 - 裝修改動了走火通道或者防火分間，但未有重新評估消防安全影響

對業主的實務建議

1. 簽租約前先查明處所的消防安全現狀，尤其是擬議用途同現有用途不一致的情況 2. 裝修工程涉及走火通道或防火分間改動，應先由專業人士評估對消防安全的影響，不好假設「裝修完先算」 3. 委聘註冊消防裝置承辦商，並確認他們的註冊類別涵蓋你需要的裝置 4. 牌照申請前，可透過相關發牌機關的諮詢程序，及早了解消防處會不會有意見，避免遞交後先發現問題

本文為一般說明性質，僅供参考，不構成專業或法律意見。具體處所的消防安全要求，應向消防處或相關發牌機關查詢，並諮詢註冊消防裝置承辦商或相關專業人士的意見。

Where FSD sits in the licensing system

FSD is not the licensing authority for most premises, but almost every type of licensed premises — restaurants, guesthouses, schools, child care centres, entertainment premises, massage establishments — requires FSD's "no objection" or certification that fire service installations and means of escape meet requirements, before the actual licensing authority issues the licence.

In other words, FSD acts as a gatekeeper within the licensing process, not the final licensing body. This distinction matters: when an owner or tenant's licence application stalls, the cause is often not the application form itself but the premises' fire safety condition falling short.

Two key documents

Certificate of Fire Service Installations and Equipment (FS 251)

New, altered or added fire service installations (fire alarms, sprinklers, hose reels) must be inspected on completion by a registered FSI Contractor (FSIC), who certifies compliance and submits FS 251 to FSD. This document is a core reference FSD checks against when a licence application is reviewed.

Fire safety requirements (vary by premises type)

FSD sets fire safety requirements specific to each premises category, covering escape route width and number, fire doors, emergency lighting and the classes of installation required. These vary by use, size and floor position — there is no single universal standard.

Registered FSI Contractors (FSIC)

Installation and maintenance of fire service installations must by law be carried out by an FSD-registered contractor (Class 1, 2 or 3 depending on installation type). When commissioning renovation or fire service works, owners should confirm the contractor's active registration on FSD's published list.

Practical effect on licence applications

Common causes of delay include escape routes obstructed by partitions or storage, installations by unregistered contractors so FS 251 cannot be validly issued, a change of use inconsistent with the original design (e.g. an industrial unit converted to a restaurant, which raises the fire safety bar), and renovation altering escape routes or compartmentation without reassessing fire safety impact.

Practical advice for owners

Check the premises' fire safety status before signing a lease, particularly where intended use differs from current use. Have renovation affecting escape routes or compartmentation assessed by a professional first, rather than assuming it can be resolved after the fact. Engage a registered FSI contractor whose registration class covers the installations you need. Where possible, use the relevant licensing authority's pre-application consultation to learn of FSD concerns before formal submission.

General guidance only, not professional or legal advice. Specific requirements should be verified with FSD or the relevant licensing authority, and a registered FSI contractor or other relevant professional consulted.
