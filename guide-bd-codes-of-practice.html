屋宇署作業備考：業主與法團最常遇到的六份技術指引Buildings Department Codes of Practice: Six Guides Owners and Corporations Actually Encounter

躍昇建築事務顧問有限公司　·　更新於 2026年7月Ascend Architecture & Engineering Ltd　·　Updated July 2026

屋宇署的作業備考（Code of Practice）數量龐大，大部分是給認可人士、工程師做設計計算用的技術規格，業主毋須逐份閱讀。但當中有幾份，會直接影響業主委聘工程、審批維修方案時看得懂的內容——認清它們的用途，勝過死記文件名稱。The Buildings Department publishes a large number of Codes of Practice, most of which are technical design references for Authorized Persons and engineers — owners don't need to read them. But a handful directly affect what owners encounter when commissioning works or approving repair proposals. Knowing what each is for matters more than memorising its title.

作業備考的性質：不是法例本身

作業備考（Code of Practice）是屋宇署根據《建築物條例》發出的技術規格文件，訂明特定範疇的設計及施工標準。它與條例本身的關係，類似前文提到的「主條例＋附屬法例」架構再往下一層：條例訂立框架，規例訂立具體法定要求，作業備考則進一步提供符合這些要求的技術方法。

對業主而言，作業備考的實務意義通常不在於自己要讀懂技術細節，而在於：知道某項工程「應該有一份對應的作業備考」，從而判斷委聘的專業人士是否按正確標準設計，以及在收到方案或報告時，知道對方引用的文件是否合理。

六份與業主日常最相關的作業備考

一、《消防安全守則》（Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings）

規管樓宇的防火結構、走火通道、消防裝置等要求。裝修工程中最常見的違規，正是拆除或改動了受這份守則規管的防火分間、走火通道闊度或防煙設施——業主裝修時若涉及間隔改動，應向專業人士確認是否觸及這份守則的規管範圍。

二、《拆卸工程守則》（Code of Practice for Demolition of Buildings）

規管拆卸工程（包括局部拆除）的安全要求。業主進行任何涉及結構的改動——即使只是拆除部分間隔或構件——都可能觸及這份守則，尤其是舊樓結構相連的情況，貿然拆除可能影響鄰近單位的結構安全。

三、《外部維修通道作業備考》（Code of Practice on Access for External Maintenance）

規管樓宇外牆維修時的通道及吊船設施要求。這份與石屎剝落維修、外牆勘察直接相關——法團進行外牆大維修時，承辦商的吊船棚架方案理應符合這份守則，業主立案法團在審批工程方案時，可以此作為其中一項檢查點。

四、《升降機及自動梯建築工程作業備考》（Code of Practice for Building Works for Lifts and Escalators）

規管升降機及自動梯相關建築工程（例如機房、井道）的設計要求。法團進行升降機更換或改造時，相關的建築工程部分須符合這份守則，與升降機本身的機電規格（受另一套機電工程法例規管）是兩回事，容易被混淆。

五、《樓宇保養手冊》（Building Maintenance Guidebook）

性質與其他技術性守則不同——這是一份面向業主及法團的實務指引，涵蓋樓宇各部分（外牆、天台、渠務、消防裝置等）的定期保養建議及檢查周期。對法團而言，這是規劃日常維修及大維修周期時最直接可用的參考文件，比其他技術性作業備考更適合業主直接查閱。

六、《小型工程監管制度指引》（General Guidelines on Minor Works Control System）

與本網站另一篇文章〈小型工程還是改建加建？〉直接對應，訂明小型工程三個級別的具體適用範圍及圖示例子。業主或裝修公司若對某項工程應循小型工程還是正式入則處理有疑問，這是判斷的技術依據。

常見混淆

升降機的建築工程（機房、井道）受《升降機及自動梯建築工程作業備考》規管，但升降機本身的機電裝置、安全掣、保養檢驗，是受另一套機電工程相關法例規管——兩者由不同專業負責，業主／法團審批方案時，宜先弄清楚眼前的建議書屬於哪一類。

對業主的實務意義

理解這批文件的存在，主要用途有三個：

1. 委聘時可以問得更準確——例如進行外牆維修時，可以問承辦商方案是否符合《外部維修通道作業備考》的要求，而不只是問「安不安全」。 2. 審批方案時多一重檢查——法團委員審批工程建議書時，若專業人士的方案有提及依據哪份作業備考，可以理解這代表方案有明確的技術依循，而非單憑經驗判斷。 3. 識別工程範圍的技術複雜度——同一項工程（例如外牆維修）可能同時涉及多份守則（外部維修通道、消防安全、結構相關守則），這正是為何委聘同時具備多項註冊資格的團隊，能夠減省業主在不同專業之間協調的原因。

版本更新頻繁，須以現行版為準

作業備考會不定期發出修訂（Amendments），部分文件近年已有多次修訂記錄。業主或法團若取得專業人士提交的技術方案，理應假設對方引用的是現行有效版本；如有疑問，可要求對方註明所依據的版本及修訂日期。

本文為一般說明性質，僅供参考，不構成專業或法律意見。各份作業備考的現行版本及修訂內容，應以屋宇署網站（www.bd.gov.hk）刊載的最新版本為準，具體工程應諮詢認可人士、註冊結構工程師或相關專業人士的意見。

What these documents are — not the ordinance itself

Codes of Practice are technical specification documents issued by the Buildings Department under the Buildings Ordinance, setting design and construction standards for specific areas. Their relationship to the Ordinance sits one level below the "Ordinance plus subsidiary regulations" structure described elsewhere: the Ordinance sets the framework, regulations set specific statutory requirements, and Codes of Practice provide the technical methods for meeting those requirements.

For owners, the practical value of these documents usually isn't in reading the technical detail yourself — it's in knowing that a given type of work "should have a corresponding Code of Practice," so you can judge whether your appointed professional is designing to the correct standard, and recognise whether a proposal or report cites something reasonable.

Six Codes most relevant to everyday ownership

1. Code of Practice for Fire Safety in Buildings

Governs fire-resisting construction, escape routes and fire service installations. The most common renovation violation is removing or altering fire compartmentation, escape route width, or smoke-proofing regulated by this Code — owners planning layout changes should confirm with a professional whether the work touches its scope.

2. Code of Practice for Demolition of Buildings

Governs the safety requirements for demolition works, including partial demolition. Any structural alteration — even removing part of a partition or element — may fall within this Code, particularly in older buildings with interconnected structures, where careless removal can affect the structural safety of neighbouring units.

3. Code of Practice on Access for External Maintenance

Governs access and gondola requirements for external wall maintenance. Directly relevant to spalling repair and external wall surveys — when a corporation carries out major external repairs, the contractor's scaffolding or gondola proposal should comply with this Code, giving committees a concrete check point when approving works.

4. Code of Practice for Building Works for Lifts and Escalators

Governs the building works associated with lifts and escalators (machine rooms, shafts). When a corporation replaces or alters lifts, the building works component must comply with this Code — distinct from the lift's electromechanical specifications, which sit under separate legislation, a distinction that is often confused.

5. Building Maintenance Guidebook

Different in nature from the other technical Codes — this is a practical guide aimed at owners and corporations, covering recommended maintenance intervals for each part of a building (external walls, roofs, drainage, fire installations). For corporations, it is the most directly usable reference for planning routine maintenance and major repair cycles.

6. General Guidelines on Minor Works Control System

Corresponds directly to our article "Minor Works or A&A?", setting out the specific scope and illustrated examples for each of the three minor works classes. This is the technical basis for judging whether a piece of work should follow the minor works route or a full submission.

A common confusion

The building works for a lift — machine room, shaft — fall under the Code of Practice for Building Works for Lifts and Escalators. But the lift's electromechanical installation, safety devices and maintenance inspection sit under separate electrical and mechanical legislation, handled by a different professional. Owners and corporations should clarify which category a proposal falls into before approving it.

Practical implications for owners

Understanding that these documents exist has three main uses: asking more precise questions when appointing a contractor (for external wall repair, asking whether the proposal complies with the Access for External Maintenance Code, not just "is it safe"); an extra check when approving proposals (a professional's proposal citing a specific Code suggests a defined technical basis rather than experience alone); and recognising the technical complexity of a job (the same external wall repair may touch several Codes at once — access, fire safety, structural — which is part of why appointing one team with multiple registrations reduces the coordination an owner would otherwise carry).

Frequent revisions — always check the current version

Codes of Practice are amended periodically, and several have multiple amendment records in recent years. Owners and corporations receiving a technical proposal should assume the professional is citing the current version; if in doubt, ask them to state which version and amendment date they relied on.

General guidance only, not professional or legal advice. Current versions and amendments are published on the Buildings Department website (www.bd.gov.hk); specific works should be referred to an Authorized Person, Registered Structural Engineer or other relevant professional.
