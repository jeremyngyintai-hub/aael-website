水務署：註冊水喉匠、喉管申請與簡樸房水錶Water Supplies Department: Licensed Plumbers, Permits and BHU Metering

躍昇建築事務顧問有限公司　·　更新於 2026年7月Ascend Architecture & Engineering Ltd　·　Updated July 2026

任何涉及喉管的工程——由簡樸房獨立水錶，到普通住宅改動廁所喉管——都受《水務設施條例》規管，而且必須由註冊水喉匠處理。這個範疇的法律要求，比好多業主想像中嚴格。Any work involving plumbing — from BHU independent water meters to altering a bathroom's pipework — is regulated by the Waterworks Ordinance and must be carried out by a licensed plumber. The legal requirements here are stricter than most owners assume.

註冊水喉匠制度

《水務設施條例》規定，喉管工程（包括安裝、更改、拆除食水內部喉管或者消防喉管）只可以由註冊水喉匠進行，其他人士（包括業主自己）進行呢類工程屬違法。這個制度已經運作接近一世紀，並非近年新增規定。

註冊水喉匠的資格由水務署審核，須通過相關考試並具備適當技術認識，包括食水內部喉管及消防喉管的相關知識。

開工前須取得書面許可

根據《水務設施條例》第14條，任何人不得在未經水務署書面許可下，建造、安裝、更改或者拆除食水內部喉管或消防喉管。程序上：

1. 由註冊水喉匠填妥申請表格（連同認可人士簽署，如涉及新水供） 2. 提交詳細的喉管建議圖則 3. 取得水務署書面許可後，方可開始施工 4. 完工後，喉管及物料須符合《水務設施規例》的要求

這個許可程序同屋宇署的入則審批是兩個獨立程序，一個處理結構同用途，一個處理喉管本身——業主容易誤以為屋宇署批了就等於水務署都批了，但實際上兩者分開處理。

與簡樸房認證的直接關係

簡樸房認證的其中一項最低標準，是單位須配備水務署提供的獨立水錶及電力公司提供的獨立電錶。呢一點特別容易被誤解：

- 由電工或者裝修師傅在單位內自行加裝的「分錶」並不符合要求——必須是水務署正式登記的獨立水錶 - 獨立水錶的申請涉及正式的喉管建議圖則審批同實地安裝，需時由數星期至數月 - 呢一項因為需要時間較長，往往是整個簡樸房認證流程入面最容易拖慢進度的一環，宜盡早處理，不好留到最後先申請

對業主的實務建議

1. 委聘工程前，確認對方持有有效的水喉匠註冊——這個可以向水務署查證 2. 涉及水管改動的裝修（例如廁所移位、加裝獨立水錶），須經正式的書面許可程序，不可以視為「小型工程」自行處理 3. 申請簡樸房獨立水錶，應盡早開始，因為呢一步的審批同安裝需時，往往決定成個認證時間表 4. 不要假設屋宇署批准等於水務署批准——兩者是獨立的審批程序

本文為一般說明性質，僅供参考，不構成專業或法律意見。具體喉管工程要求應向水務署查詢，並委聘註冊水喉匠及相關專業人士處理。

The licensed plumber system

The Waterworks Ordinance requires that plumbing work — installing, altering or removing an inside service (fresh water) or fire service pipework — be carried out only by a licensed plumber. This system has operated for close to a century; it is not a recent addition.

Licensed plumber status is assessed by WSD through examination and requires adequate knowledge of both inside service and fire service plumbing.

Written permission required before work starts

Under Section 14 of the Waterworks Ordinance, no person may construct, install, alter or remove an inside service or fire service without WSD's written permission. The process: a licensed plumber completes the application form (co-signed by an Authorized Person where new water supply is involved), submits detailed plumbing proposal drawings, obtains written permission before commencing, and on completion the pipes and materials must comply with the Waterworks Regulations.

This permission process is separate from the Buildings Department's plan approval — one governs structure and use, the other governs the plumbing itself. Owners often assume BD approval covers water supply too; it does not.

Direct relevance to BHU certification

One of the Basic Housing Unit minimum standards is that the unit be fitted with an individual water meter provided by WSD and an individual electricity meter from the power company. This point is commonly misunderstood:

A "sub-meter" installed by an electrician or contractor inside the unit does not satisfy the requirement — it must be a WSD-registered individual meter. Applying for one involves formal approval of plumbing proposal drawings and physical installation, taking weeks to months. Because of this lead time, it is usually the single most common cause of delay in the whole BHU certification process, and should be started early rather than left until last.

Practical advice for owners

Confirm a contractor's active plumber's licence before engaging them — this can be verified with WSD. Renovation involving pipework changes (relocating a bathroom, adding an individual meter) requires the formal written permission process; it cannot be treated as a self-managed minor job. Start the individual water meter application for BHU certification as early as possible, since its approval and installation timeline often sets the pace for the whole certification. Do not assume BD approval implies WSD approval — they are separate processes.

General guidance only, not professional or legal advice. Specific plumbing requirements should be verified with WSD, with work carried out by a licensed plumber and other relevant professionals.
